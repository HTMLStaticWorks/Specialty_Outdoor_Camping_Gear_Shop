QUALITY STANDARDS

WHAT WE LOOK FOR IN GEAR

Five core characteristics defined for long-term trail usability.

DURABILITY

High-tenacity ripstop fabrics and reinforced stress points built for rough rock and thorn brush.

PACKABILITY

High warmth-to-weight hydrophobic down and compact nesting titanium pots that stow efficiently.

USABILITY

Glove-friendly zipper pulls, intuitive color-coded tent poles, and quick-adjust harness straps.

WEATHER READINESS

Taped waterproof seams, storm vestibules, and breathable hard-shell membranes.

REPAIRABILITY

Modular pole segments, replaceable hip belts, and patchable tent fabric designs.
