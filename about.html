HERITAGE & ROOTS

OUR STORY: FORGED IN THE CASCADES

Trailforge was born out of cold ridge bivouacs, saturated down sleeping bags, and broken tent poles in unforecast alpine storms. Founded in 2014 by lifelong backpackers and thru-hikers in the Pacific Northwest, our mission started in a modest gear-tuning garage with one objective: to build and curate equipment that never leaves you stranded when weather turns severe.

We realized that the outdoor market had become saturated with mass-market consumer camping gear—slickly marketed for backyard sunny weekends, but prone to seam leaks, plastic buckle breaks, and thermal collapse on real wilderness traverses. We set out to create the exact opposite: specialized, field-engineered gear that backpackers can stake their lives on.

THE TURNING POINT

WHY WE STARTED TRAILFORGE

A November ridge blizzard exposed the fatal flaw of disposable consumer gear and sparked our purpose-built revolution.

THE PROBLEM WE FACED

THE EXPEDITION BREAKING POINT

In late autumn 2013, our founding crew was trapped on an exposed ridge at 8,200 feet along the northern Cascades when an unanticipated blizzard rolled in. A commercially marketed "four-season" tent snapped an alloy pole at the ferrule joint, while a sleeping pad valve froze open at 2:00 AM in 12°F temperatures.

We survived using duct tape, ski pole splints, and emergency bivvies. That night taught us an unforgettable lesson: when gear fails in remote backcountry, there is no technical support or replacement store. Cheap manufacturing had infiltrated the outdoor market.

⚠️
THE INDUSTRY FLAW Fragile alloys, glued seams that delaminate, and planned obsolescence designed for landfills.
OUR SOLUTION

THE TRAILFORGE STANDARD

We founded Trailforge to build the equipment we wished we had on that frozen ridge. We abandoned gimmicks and committed to four non-negotiable standards: 40D diamond-ripstop fabrics, aircraft-grade DAC aluminum poles, modular hardware that can be serviced in the field, and genuine shivering-limit temperature testing.

Every piece in our collection is designed with field repairability at its core. If a zipper slider wears down after 500 nights outside, or a pole segment bends, it can be repaired in minutes—keeping your gear on the trail for decades.
